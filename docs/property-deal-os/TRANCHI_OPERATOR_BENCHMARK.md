## Executive choice
**Do not clone 200 agents.** Build a small set of real, testable domain capabilities integrated with the existing X1 queue, safe scheduler, MCP, data cleaner and Eden communications. Each capability must earn promotion through source provenance, deterministic mathematics, role-specific QA, provider receipts and demonstrated value to the two licensed-agent partners. Tranchi's pricing is custom and currently unverified; no purchase justified solely by claimed feature count.

## Priority sequence
- **P0, commercial proof:** Repair Supabase source pinning, fresh Broward listings, safe Data Cleaner, accurate contract-flip scenario screening, buyer buy-boxes, qualified partner review, daily evidence-based feed and deal ledger.
- **P1, execution readiness:** REO/tax deed monitoring, DSCR and BRRRR screening, title-partner request workflow, AI-assisted draft negotiations, existing Eden communications with verified permissions, transaction coordinator and fully tested MCP read/draft tools.
- **P2, coverage expansion:** government-seized, sheriff/probate, commercial/multifamily, financing scenarios, geospatial signals, agent browser, expert reference assistant, professional directory and seller financing.
- **P3, partnerships:** real lending/broker relationships, property-management and tenant features after fair-housing checks, coaching/education, white label and market expansion.

## Measurable acceptance
1. Five actual authorized fresh Broward properties with unique parcel IDs, evidence URLs and verification timestamps; no destructive or same-ZIP merges.
2. Ten deterministic underwriting fixtures including negative profits, missing data, DSCR zero debt-service, BRRRR refinance sensitivity, seller-financing terms, and cap-rate assumptions.
3. One high-priority opportunity visible in the authorized agent review queue with buyer-buy-box match evidence, source freshness, required checks and nonbinding next-step draft.
4. Reusable signed/authenticated MCP tool list/readback and negative tenant tests; no credentials in client, GitHub, plugin or logs.
5. Verified consent, STOP, channel registration and real provider receipts before any live SMS or AI outbound voice; no 250-contact automatic campaign.
6. Two productive scheduled ingestion cycles, tested recovery and one independently approved release SHA. Revenue only from verified title/bank closing receipts or separately proven software payments.

## Regulatory and product design gates
- Florida Chapter 475 and each responsible supervising broker determine compensated brokerage and assignment activities; attorney to approve transaction templates, fee structure and disclosures.
- FCC's AI-voice classification under TCPA makes outbound AI calls subject to restrictions and consent. Cold calling at marketed volume is not a deliverable without demonstrated lawful authorization, registered provider compliance, do-not-call filtering and recipient consent.
- Tenant placement and screening must avoid protected-trait inference or discriminatory ranking, including disability/familial-status proxies; apply relevant fair-housing and voucher rules.
- No false title-clear, bank-backing, government-property access, lender eligibility, investment return, coach certification or guaranteed income claims.
- Compare products fairly; no automated scraping of Tranchi's gated application, proprietary material, authentication or customer data.
